TruckDrop — Privacy Notice (Draft)

Last updated: 2025-10-29

This is a short, user-facing privacy notice for TruckDrop. It is a draft you can include in your store listing or link from the app's Settings/All Done screen.

Summary

TruckDrop respects your privacy. The game does not collect personal information by default. We only collect minimal, anonymous information if you explicitly opt in (for example, to help improve gameplay or to send anonymous crash reports).

What we may collect

  • Local data: game progress, settings, and in‑game coins are stored on the device (localStorage) and are not transmitted off the device unless you explicitly export or share them.
  • Ads: TruckDrop may display ads (e.g. Google AdMob). Ad networks may collect device and usage data required to serve ads. Ad personalization can be disabled in the game's settings or by declining consent if a consent prompt is shown.
  • Optional diagnostics/analytics: If you enable “help improve the game” or agree to anonymous diagnostics, the game may send anonymous usage events and crash reports (no names, no contact info) to help debug issues.

Third parties

We may use third‑party services to display ads or provide analytics and diagnostics. These services operate under their own privacy terms. Examples include:

  • Google AdMob (ads) — ad networks may collect identifiers and interaction data.
  • Analytics / crash reporting providers (if enabled) — capture anonymous error reports and event counts.

We do not share personal information with third parties unless you explicitly request it (for example, by sharing a bug report or contacting support).

Your choices

  • Decline analytics/diagnostics: you can choose not to enable anonymous analytics or crash reporting. If declined, no telemetry is uploaded.
  • Ad personalization: you may be able to disable personalized ads in Settings (the option will be shown at first launch if applicable). Non‑personalized ads may still be shown.
  • Delete data: progress and settings are stored locally. To delete them, clear the app storage or use the in‑game “Reset progress” option if provided.

Data retention

Anonymous diagnostics and analytics (if enabled) are retained only as long as needed to analyze and fix issues. Exact retention depends on the service provider's defaults.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, want to request deletion of any data we control, or need more details, contact: dev@sir.ev (replace with a valid contact email before publishing).

Notes for publishing

  • Do not include secrets (API keys or DSNs) in the repository. Use environment variables or your CI to supply them for production builds.
  • When you publish to Google Play or the App Store, accurately fill out their data safety / privacy forms and include a link to your privacy policy.
